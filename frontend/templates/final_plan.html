🗣️ 02 · HR Questions to Revise

Tell me about yourself.
What are your strengths?
What is your weakness?
Why should we hire you?
Why do you want to join our company?
Where do you see yourself in five years?
Tell me about your project.
What challenges did you face?

🚀 03 · Project Preparation

Be ready to explain these five things:

1. What problem does your project solve?
2. Why did you choose this project?
3. What technologies did you use?
4. What challenges did you face?
5. What would you improve in the future?

👔 04 · Interview Etiquette

Dress professionally.
Reach the interview location early.
Maintain comfortable eye contact.
Sit straight and maintain confident body language.
Listen carefully before answering.
Speak clearly and avoid rushing.

🚫 05 · What NOT To Do

❌ Don't memorize every answer word-for-word.

❌ Don't interrupt the interviewer.

❌ Don't give extremely long answers.

❌ Don't speak negatively about previous experiences.

❌ Don't pretend to know something you don't know.

❌ Don't panic if you don't know one question.

🗣️ 06 · Communication Checklist

I can introduce myself in 60–90 seconds.
I can explain my project clearly.
I can explain my technical concepts in simple English.
I can answer why I want this job.
I can talk about my strengths and weakness.

🌙 07 · One Day Before Interview

Complete a final revision instead of learning completely new topics.
Keep your resume ready.
Check interview time and location.
Keep required documents ready.
Prepare your clothes and essentials.
Get enough sleep.

☀️ 08 · Interview Day

🌟 Stay calm.

🌟 Listen carefully.

🌟 Take a few seconds to think before answering.

🌟 If you don't know an answer, be honest.

🌟 Focus on communicating what you know.

🌟 Remember: one difficult question does not define your entire interview.

🏆 Final Confidence Reminder

You don't need to know everything to perform well in an interview.

Show your willingness to learn, communicate clearly and explain what you genuinely know.

Prepare well. Stay calm. Trust yourself.
