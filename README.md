# 🎯 Placement Navigator AI

### From Placement Announcement to Interview Readiness

Placement Navigator AI is an AI-powered placement preparation platform designed to help students prepare for campus placements in a structured and personalized way.

Instead of searching for preparation materials separately, students can select their **company, role, interview date, and preparation level** to receive a personalized roadmap for becoming interview-ready.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🏢 Company Intelligence

Get company-specific placement information, interview patterns, and preparation guidance.

### 🗺️ Personalized Preparation Roadmap

Generate a preparation plan based on:

* Company
* Job Role
* Interview Date
* Preparation Level

### 🧠 Aptitude Preparation

Practice important aptitude and reasoning topics such as:

* Number Series
* Percentages
* Logical Reasoning
* Data Interpretation

### 💻 Technical Preparation

Prepare for technical interview topics including:

* Python
* SQL
* Excel
* Data Analysis
* Programming Fundamentals

### 📄 Resume Analysis

Analyze your resume and identify important areas that may need improvement before applying for a job.

### 🗣️ HR Preparation

Learn how to confidently answer common HR interview questions and improve your self-introduction.

### 🤖 AI Mock Interview

Practice interview questions through a simulated interview experience and identify weak areas.

### 📊 Readiness Score

Get an overall readiness score based on your preparation progress.

### ⚡ Last-Minute Preparation

Get a focused preparation plan for the final hours before an interview.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The main goal of Placement Navigator AI is to reduce the confusion and stress students face during campus placements.

The platform brings company research, preparation planning, technical learning, aptitude practice, resume preparation, HR preparation, mock interviews, and readiness tracking into one interactive system.
